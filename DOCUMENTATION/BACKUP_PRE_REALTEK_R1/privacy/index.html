This Privacy Policy describes how REALTEK ENGINEERING PTE. LTD. 1. Corporate Identity & Data Fiduciary

REALTEK ENGINEERING PTE. LTD. is a company incorporated in the Republic of Singapore under Unique Entity Number (UEN) 201723665M with registered address at 132 Gul Circle, Singapore 629597. We are the legal entity operating ezy SaaS and ezyHR, committed to upholding the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and international data privacy standards.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal data in the following contexts:

  • Website Visitors & Analytics: IP address, device browser type, access timestamps, page view interactions, cookies, and technical log data.

3. Purpose of Processing Personal Data

We collect and use personal data for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Providing engineering project quotes, customer support, and administrative communications.
  • Operating, maintaining, analytics monitoring, and securing our ezy SaaS products and customer accounts.
  • Sending service updates, transactional email notifications, and responding to inquiries.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations, audit duties, and regulatory statutory reporting requirements in Singapore.

4. Data Protection, Retention & Security

We implement strict organizational and technical security measures—including TLS encryption in transit, strict access controls, secure cloud infrastructure, and routine vulnerability assessments—to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected or to satisfy statutory legal requirements.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We share data only with essential third-party service infrastructure providers (such as web hosting, cloud infrastructure, and email delivery services) bound by strict data processing and confidentiality agreements.

Under the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or withdrawal of consent for your personal data held by us.
