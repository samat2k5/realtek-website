Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how REALTEK ENGINEERING PTE. LTD. (“Realtek”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website at https://www.realtekengg.com/, communicate with us, or utilize our technology products and cloud services under the EZY SaaS initiative (including ezyHR at https://hr.ezy.sg/).

1. Corporate Identity & Data Fiduciary

REALTEK ENGINEERING PTE. LTD. is a company incorporated in the Republic of Singapore with registered address at 132 Gul Circle, Singapore 629597. We are the legal entity operating EZY SaaS and ezyHR, committed to upholding the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and international data privacy standards.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal data in the following contexts:

  • Inquiries & Contact Forms: Name, business email address, phone number, company name, and service inquiry details submitted via email, WhatsApp, or contact forms.
  • EZY SaaS Account Administration: Account login credentials, administrative user contacts, and billing/subscription information for ezyHR users.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, access timestamps, and page interaction metrics for website performance optimization.

3. Purpose of Processing Personal Data

We collect and use personal data for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Providing engineering project quotes, customer support, and administrative communications.
  • Operating, maintaining, and securing our EZY SaaS products and customer accounts.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations and regulatory statutory reporting requirements in Singapore.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement strict organizational and technical security measures—including TLS encryption, access controls, secure hosting infrastructure, and routine system updates—to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share data with trusted service infrastructure providers (such as cloud hosting and email delivery services) bound by strict confidentiality and data protection obligations.

6. Your Rights & Access Requests

Subject to Singapore PDPA provisions, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or withdrawal of consent for your personal data stored by us.
