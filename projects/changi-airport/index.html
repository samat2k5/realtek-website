Flagship Project

Changi Airport Terminal 1 & Terminal 2

High-security commercial and industrial solar photovoltaic installation across critical aviation infrastructure.

Operational Context

Executing renewable energy infrastructure within Changi Airport Terminals 1 and 2 required adherence to stringent aviation security protocols, zero-downtime operational mandates, and highly coordinated logistics.

Engineering Scope

Realtek Engineering Pte. Ltd. was contracted to deploy a massive-scale metal roof PV system. The scope encompassed the structural mounting, DC/AC cable routing across complex terminal architecture, and the integration of inverters into the existing high-voltage electrical grid.

Execution & Delivery

Our deployment teams operated under restricted access conditions, completing the installation of 7,080 photovoltaic panels without disrupting airport operations.

Technical Specifications
Location Changi Airport, Singapore
System Capacity 4,141.8 kWp
Array Size 7,080 PV Panels
Installation Type Metal Roof PV
