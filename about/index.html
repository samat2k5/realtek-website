About REALTEK ENGINEERING PTE. LTD.

Singapore Engineering & Technology Solutions

REALTEK ENGINEERING PTE. LTD. is a Singapore-incorporated company providing integrated Solar Energy, Electrical & Instrumentation (E&I), Digital Solutions, and Cloud SaaS business applications.

Built on Engineering. Driven by Technology.

REALTEK ENGINEERING PTE. LTD. combines traditional industrial engineering capability with modern cloud technology to deliver reliable solutions for commercial and industrial clients in Singapore.

Core Engineering Capabilities

Realtek Engineering specializes in multi-disciplinary industrial engineering services, including:

  • Solar & Sustainability: Large-scale industrial rooftop solar panel installations, high-voltage cabling routing, structural mounting, and solar maintenance.
  • Electrical & Instrumentation (E&I): Control panel assembly, industrial cable tray routing, power distribution, and precision instrumentation controls.
  • Digital Solutions: Custom enterprise software engineering, web application development, and industrial digital transformation.

Technology Division — EZY SaaS

As part of our continuing operational expansion and technology innovation, REALTEK ENGINEERING PTE. LTD. develops and operates cloud-based business software under the EZY SaaS initiative.

EZY SaaS focuses on building practical, secure, and scalable cloud business applications that streamline everyday business management.

Flagship SaaS Product — ezyHR

ezyHR (`https://hr.ezy.sg/`) is our first active SaaS product. It is a cloud-based HRMS and payroll platform designed for Singapore businesses, with features to support local payroll and HR administration requirements.
