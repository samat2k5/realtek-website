9. CRITICAL ezyHR Wording Change
CURRENT LIVE WORDING TO REMOVE/REPLACE:
“Comprehensive Singapore statutory payroll, leave management, and employee self-service platform.”
Reason: this wording can be read as a strong statutory/compliance claim. Until the applicable IRAS/AIS status is formally established, avoid wording that could imply certification, approval, endorsement or official compliance status.
RECOMMENDED REPLACEMENT:
“Cloud-based HRMS and payroll platform designed for Singapore businesses.”
Optional longer version:
“Cloud-based HRMS and payroll platform designed for Singapore businesses, with features to support local payroll and HR administration requirements.”
Never use “IRAS Certified”, “IRAS Approved”, “IRAS Endorsed” or equivalent unless the legal/compliance team has confirmed the exact status and wording.
Only advertise features that exist in the production application.
