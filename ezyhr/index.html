EZY SAAS PRODUCT

ezyHR

Cloud HRMS & Payroll for Singapore Businesses

Simplify HR. Streamline Payroll. Empower Your People.

ezyHR is a cloud-based Human Resource Management System developed by REALTEK ENGINEERING PTE. LTD., Singapore. Built specifically to handle modern workforce administration, leave workflows, attendance tracking, and statutory payroll considerations.

Visit ezyHR Portal → Contact ezyHR Support
CORE CAPABILITIES

Key Platform Features

Designed to streamline every aspect of Singapore business HR and payroll management.

👤

Employee Management

Centralised employee profiles, employment history, digital contract storage, and comprehensive HR records.

📱

Employee Self-Service

Empower employees with direct mobile and web access to view payslips, submit leave applications, and update personal details.

⏰

Attendance & Roster

Track employee working hours, clock-in records, shift rosters, and overtime calculations seamlessly.

📅

Leave Management

Automated leave applications, multi-tier manager approvals, real-time balances, and holiday calendars.

💰

Payroll Engine

Structured payroll processing, itemised electronic payslips, CPF calculations, and automated pay summaries.

🧾

Claims & Expenses

Streamlined expense claim submissions with receipt uploads and multi-level manager approval workflows.

📊

HR & Payroll Reports

Comprehensive analytical reporting and exportable summaries for management, finance, and auditing needs.

🇸🇬

Singapore-focused

Designed specifically around Singapore business practices, statutory guidelines, and AIS payroll submission requirements.
