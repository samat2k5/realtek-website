About REALTEK ENGINEERING PTE. LTD.

Singapore Engineering & Technology Solutions

REALTEK ENGINEERING PTE. LTD. is a Singapore-based engineering and technology company providing Solar, Electrical & Instrumentation, Digital Solutions and technology services to businesses.

Who is Realtek?

REALTEK ENGINEERING PTE. LTD. is a Singapore-incorporated company providing integrated Solar Energy, Electrical & Instrumentation (E&I), Digital Solutions, and Cloud SaaS business applications. We combine traditional industrial engineering capability with modern cloud technology to deliver reliable solutions for commercial and industrial clients in Singapore.

What is ezy SaaS?

ezy SaaS is the technology software initiative of REALTEK ENGINEERING PTE. LTD. As part of our operational expansion and technology innovation, ezy SaaS focuses on building practical, secure, and scalable cloud business applications that streamline everyday business management.

What is ezyHR?

ezyHR (`https://hr.ezy.sg/`) is the first active SaaS product developed and operated through the ezy SaaS initiative. It is a cloud-based HRMS and payroll platform designed for Singapore businesses, with features to support local payroll and HR administration requirements.

Core Engineering Capabilities

  • Solar & Sustainability: Large-scale industrial rooftop solar panel installations, high-voltage cabling routing, structural mounting, and system maintenance.
  • Electrical & Instrumentation (E&I): Control panel assembly, industrial cable tray routing, power distribution, and precision instrumentation controls.
  • Digital Solutions: Custom enterprise software engineering, web application development, and industrial digital transformation.
